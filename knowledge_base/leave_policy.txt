DOCUMENT: COMPREHENSIVE LEAVE AND ATTENDANCE POLICY

==================================================
SECTION: ANNUAL PAID LEAVE (PTO)
==================================================
- Entitlement: Full-time employees receive 22 days of paid time off per calendar year.
- Accrual: PTO accrues on a monthly basis at 1.83 days per completed month of active service.
- Carryover Rules: A maximum of 8 unused vacation days can be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year. Unused days beyond 8 will lapse automatically on December 31st.
- Advance Notice: Planned leaves of 3 or more consecutive days require at least 1 week advance manager notification.

==================================================
SECTION: CASUAL AND SICK LEAVE
==================================================
- Allocation: 10 days of paid casual and sick leave per year, credited in advance on January 1st.
- Consecutive Sick Days: If sick leave extends beyond 3 consecutive working days, a formal medical practitioner certificate must be uploaded to HRMS.
- Carryover: Casual and sick leaves do not carry forward and cannot be encashed upon separation.

==================================================
SECTION: MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
==================================================
- Maternity Leave: Female employees are eligible for 26 weeks (182 calendar days) of fully paid maternity leave for up to two surviving children. Leave can begin up to 8 weeks prior to the expected delivery date.
- Paternity Leave: Male employees are entitled to 15 working days of fully paid paternity leave, usable within the first 6 months of childbirth.
- Adoption Leave: Primary adopting parents receive 16 weeks of paid leave; secondary adopting parents receive 4 weeks of paid leave.

==================================================
SECTION: BEREAVEMENT AND COMPASSIONATE LEAVE
==================================================
- Duration: 5 consecutive working days of paid leave upon the passing of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, or parent-in-law).
- Additional Days: Managers may approve up to 3 additional unpaid or vacation days if travel is necessary.

==================================================
SECTION: LEAVE APPLICATION AND APPROVAL WORKFLOW
==================================================
- Submission: All leave requests must be submitted through the self-service HRMS mobile app or web portal.
- Approval SLA: Managers must approve or reject leave requests within 48 business hours.
- Emergency Leave: In case of unexpected illness, notify your team lead via Slack or email by 9:30 AM local time on the day of absence.
