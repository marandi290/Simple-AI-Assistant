DOCUMENT: HIRING PROCESS AND RECRUITING GUIDELINES

==================================================
SECTION: OVERVIEW AND PHILOSOPHY
==================================================
The hiring process at Apex Technologies is designed to evaluate technical depth, problem-solving capability, and alignment with our collaborative culture. We prioritize objective, structured interviews and seek candidates who demonstrate curiosity, resilience, and strong communication skills.

==================================================
SECTION: STAGES OF INTERVIEW PROCESS
==================================================
1. Recruiter Screen (30 mins): Initial discussion covering candidate background, career goals, role expectations, and compensation alignment.
2. Technical Screen (60 mins): Live coding and problem-solving assessment focusing on data structures, algorithms, and practical debugging.
3. System Design and Architecture (60 mins): For mid-to-senior levels, evaluates system scalability, database trade-offs, caching, and distributed systems.
4. Hiring Manager Round (45 mins): Discussion on past projects, technical leadership, cross-functional collaboration, and team dynamics.
5. Values and Culture Fit (45 mins): Behavioral interview assessing alignment with company values, conflict resolution, and feedback receptivity.

==================================================
SECTION: CANDIDATE EVALUATION AND SCORING
==================================================
- Standardized Rubrics: Every interviewer must complete a structured scorecard in the applicant tracking system within 24 hours of the interview.
- Scoring Scale: Strong Hire, Hire, Leaning Hire, Leaning No Hire, Strong No Hire.
- Debrief Meeting: A 30-minute debrief is scheduled if there are split recommendations among the hiring panel.

==================================================
SECTION: OFFER APPROVAL AND EXTENSION
==================================================
- Compensation Banding: Offers are benchmarked against industry percentiles to maintain internal equity across levels.
- Background Verification: All candidate offers are contingent upon clear background verification (education, employment history, and criminal record check).
- Formal Extension: The hiring manager and recruiter conduct an offer call followed by sending the official digital offer letter via DocuSign.

==================================================
SECTION: ONBOARDING HANDOVER
==================================================
Once an offer is accepted:
1. Recruiter initiates the pre-onboarding workflow in the HR portal.
2. IT receives automated notification to configure and dispatch the employee's workstation.
3. Hiring Manager assigns an onboarding buddy to guide the new hire during their first 90 days.
