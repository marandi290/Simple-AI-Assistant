DOCUMENT: MEDICAL INSURANCE AND HEALTH BENEFIT POLICY

==================================================
SECTION: GROUP HEALTH INSURANCE (GHI)
==================================================
- Base Coverage: Apex Technologies provides comprehensive Group Health Insurance covering the employee, spouse, and up to two dependent children (up to age 25).
- Sum Insured: Family floater sum insured of INR 10,00,000 for India-based employees ($250,000 for US-based employees).
- Premium: 100% of the base insurance premium is covered by the company.
- Parental Insurance Top-Up: Employees have the voluntary option to enroll parents or in-laws via payroll deduction during annual open enrollment in November.

==================================================
SECTION: COVERAGE DETAILS AND EXCLUSIONS
==================================================
- Day-One Coverage: Pre-existing ailments and maternity complications are covered from the first day of employment without waiting periods.
- Room Rent Cap: Single Private Air-Conditioned room with no proportional deduction on associated medical charges.
- Daycare Treatments: Over 400 daycare procedures (e.g., dialysis, chemotherapy, cataract surgery) not requiring 24-hour hospitalization are covered.
- Exclusions: Cosmetic surgeries, experimental treatments, and unprescribed supplements are excluded.

==================================================
SECTION: OUTPATIENT (OPD) AND DENTAL REIMBURSEMENT
==================================================
- OPD Allowance: Annual outpatient consultation allowance of INR 15,000 ($500) per family unit.
- Reimbursable Services: Doctor consultation fees, prescription diagnostic tests (blood tests, X-rays, MRI scans), and prescribed medicines.
- Dental and Vision: Routine checkups, cleaning, dental fillings, prescription eyeglasses, and contact lenses qualify under the OPD benefit limit.

==================================================
SECTION: CLAIMS SUBMISSION AND REIMBURSEMENT PROCESS
==================================================
- Cashless Hospitalization: Present your MediBuddy / TPA e-card at network hospitals at least 48 hours before planned admission or within 24 hours of emergency admission.
- Reimbursement Claims: For non-network hospitals, submit original itemized discharge summaries, doctor prescriptions, and hospital bills within 30 days of discharge.
- Processing Turnaround: Approved reimbursement claims are disbursed to the employee's bank account within 10 business days.

==================================================
SECTION: ANNUAL EXECUTIVE HEALTH CHECKUP
==================================================
- Eligibility: All full-time employees who have completed 6 months of tenure are eligible for an annual preventative health screening.
- Diagnostic Partners: Available at zero out-of-pocket cost at certified partner diagnostic centers (Apollo, Metropolis, Quest Diagnostics).
- Scope of Tests: Includes Complete Blood Count (CBC), lipid profile, liver function, kidney function, HbA1c, ECG, and physician consultation.
